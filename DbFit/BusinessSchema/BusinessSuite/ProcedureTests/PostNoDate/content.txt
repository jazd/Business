Three-argument Post omits the date.

Debit is the first account. Credit is the third.

!|Query|SELECT (Post('Cash', 100, 'Sales')).entry AS entry|

!|Query|SELECT s.value AS name, CASE WHEN je.credit THEN 'credit' ELSE 'debit' END AS side FROM JournalEntry je JOIN AccountName an ON an.account = je.account JOIN Sentence s ON s.id = an.name AND s.culture = 1033 WHERE je.entry = _:eid::INT ORDER BY je.credit, s.value|
|name|side|
|Cash|debit|
|Sales|credit|

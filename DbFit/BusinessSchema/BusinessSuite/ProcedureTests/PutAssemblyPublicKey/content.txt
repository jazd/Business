PutAssemblyPublicKey

Soft-stops any active public key for the assembly, then inserts a new PEM.

Assembly from existing Part helpers
!|Query|SELECT GetPartbySerial(GetPart('ESP32-S3-WROOM'), 'TEST-MACS-001')|
|getpartbyserial?|
|>>asm|

First key
!|Execute|SELECT PutAssemblyPublicKey(_:asm::INT, '-----BEGIN PUBLIC KEY-----TESTKEY1-----END PUBLIC KEY-----')|

!|Query|SELECT COUNT(*) AS n FROM AssemblyPublicKey WHERE assembly = _:asm::INT AND stop IS NULL|
|n|
|1|

!|Query|SELECT pem FROM AssemblyPublicKey WHERE assembly = _:asm::INT AND stop IS NULL|
|pem|
|-----BEGIN PUBLIC KEY-----TESTKEY1-----END PUBLIC KEY-----|

Replace key (prior row stopped)
!|Execute|SELECT PutAssemblyPublicKey(_:asm::INT, '-----BEGIN PUBLIC KEY-----TESTKEY2-----END PUBLIC KEY-----')|

!|Query|SELECT COUNT(*) AS active FROM AssemblyPublicKey WHERE assembly = _:asm::INT AND stop IS NULL|
|active|
|1|

!|Query|SELECT COUNT(*) AS stopped FROM AssemblyPublicKey WHERE assembly = _:asm::INT AND stop IS NOT NULL|
|stopped|
|1|

!|Query|SELECT pem FROM AssemblyPublicKey WHERE assembly = _:asm::INT AND stop IS NULL|
|pem|
|-----BEGIN PUBLIC KEY-----TESTKEY2-----END PUBLIC KEY-----|

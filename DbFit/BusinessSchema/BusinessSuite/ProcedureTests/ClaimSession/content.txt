ClaimSession

Anonymous session, token, Home path. Claim finds/creates individual and unrevoked
credential, SetSession (IndividualSessionCreated), copies SessionPath, leaves
SessionPath in place. ListSubscribe is not called.

!|Query|SELECT AnonymousSession('Mozilla/5.0 (X11; Linux x86_64) AppleWebKit/537.36 (KHTML, like Gecko) Chrome/43.0.2357.130 Safari/537.36', 'Chrome', '43', '0', '2357', '130', 'Linux', NULL,NULL,NULL,NULL,NULL, 'Other', 0, 'www.ibm.com', NULL,NULL,'107.77.97.52')|
|anonymoussession?|
|>>sid|

!|Execute|INSERT INTO SessionToken (session, token) VALUES (_:sid::BIGINT, 'ClaimSessionTok')|

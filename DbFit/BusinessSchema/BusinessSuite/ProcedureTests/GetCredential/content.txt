GetCredential

An unrevoked credential for this individual and email. A second call returns the same id.

!|Query|SELECT GetIndividualEmail('dbfit-getcred@example.com')|
|getindividualemail?|
|>>ind|

!|Query|SELECT GetEmail('dbfit-getcred@example.com')|
|getemail?|
|>>em|

!|Query|SELECT GetCredential(_:ind::BIGINT, _:em::INT)|
|getcredential?|
|>>cred|

!|Query|SELECT GetCredential(_:ind::BIGINT, _:em::INT)|
|getcredential|
|<<cred|

GetIndividualEmail

Returns the Individual linked to an email (creates individual + association if needed).
Static: IndividualEmail links individual 3 to Email id 1 (Steve+NoCRUD@Jazd.com shape).

!|Query|SELECT GetIndividualEmail('Steve+NoCRUD@Jazd.com')|
|getindividualemail|
|3|

Same email again is stable
!|Query|SELECT GetIndividualEmail('Steve+NoCRUD@Jazd.com')|
|getindividualemail|
|3|

New email gets a new individual (not one of the static low ids)
!|Query|SELECT GetIndividualEmail('ZippyBringUp@example.test')|
|getindividualemail?|
|>>newind|

!|Query|SELECT GetIndividualEmail('ZippyBringUp@example.test')|
|getindividualemail|
|<<newind|

!|Query|SELECT _:newind::BIGINT > 13 AS after_static|
|after_static|
|true|

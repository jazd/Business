PutCertificateSigningRequest and PutAssemblyCertificateSigningRequest

Minimal CA / CAPolicy for FK on certificates is not required for CSR tables.

!|Query|SELECT GetPartbySerial(GetPart('ESP32-S3-WROOM'), 'TEST-CSR-ASM-001')|
|getpartbyserial?|
|>>asm|

!|Query|SELECT GetIndividualEmail('Steve+NoCRUD@Jazd.com')|
|getindividualemail|
|3|

Insert CSR
!|Query|SELECT PutCertificateSigningRequest('-----BEGIN CERTIFICATE REQUEST-----TESTCSR1-----END CERTIFICATE REQUEST-----', 3)|
|putcertificatesigningrequest?|
|>>csr|

!|Query|SELECT pem, individual FROM CertificateSigningRequest WHERE id = _:csr::INT|
|pem|individual|
|-----BEGIN CERTIFICATE REQUEST-----TESTCSR1-----END CERTIFICATE REQUEST-----|3|

Link assembly to CSR
!|Execute|SELECT PutAssemblyCertificateSigningRequest(_:asm::INT, _:csr::INT)|

!|Query|SELECT COUNT(*) AS n FROM AssemblyCertificateSigningRequest WHERE assembly = _:asm::INT AND stop IS NULL|
|n|
|1|

!|Query|SELECT csr FROM AssemblyCertificateSigningRequest WHERE assembly = _:asm::INT AND stop IS NULL|
|csr|
|<<csr|

Second CSR link soft-stops the first association
!|Query|SELECT PutCertificateSigningRequest('-----BEGIN CERTIFICATE REQUEST-----TESTCSR2-----END CERTIFICATE REQUEST-----', 3)|
|putcertificatesigningrequest?|
|>>csr2|

!|Execute|SELECT PutAssemblyCertificateSigningRequest(_:asm::INT, _:csr2::INT)|
